A Letter I Saved For The End ❤️

My Dearest Baccha ❤️

If you're reading this, it means you've reached the end of this little website.

But the truth is, this was never really about a website.

It was about us.

It was about a journey that started on 08 January 2020 and slowly became one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

When we first met at Joy Sir's tuition, I had absolutely no idea that one day I would be sitting here creating an entire website for you.

Life is strange that way.

Sometimes the most important people enter our lives quietly, and before we realise it, they become a part of our everyday happiness.

Over the years, we've shared so many conversations, memories, laughs, arguments, celebrations, and countless moments that only we understand.

Some memories are huge.

Some are very small.

But every single one of them matters to me because you were there.

One memory that will always stay close to my heart is our time in Ahmedabad.

Celebrating your birthday, spending time together, creating memories without worrying about anything else — those are moments I genuinely treasure.

Whenever I look back at our journey, I don't only remember the big occasions.

I remember the random conversations.

The unnecessary jokes.

The silly moments.

The times when a simple text from you could instantly make my day better.

Thank you for being patient with me.

Thank you for understanding me.

Thank you for supporting me during different phases of life.

Thank you for staying.

You have brought happiness into my life in ways that I don't always express properly.

And maybe that's why I wanted to create this surprise.

Because sometimes there are things that are easier to write than to say.

As you celebrate your 23rd birthday today, I want you to know how grateful I am for every memory we've created together.

I wish you success.

I wish you happiness.

I wish you peace.

I wish you every beautiful thing that life has to offer.

And no matter where life takes us in the future, there is one thing that will never change:

The place that these memories hold in my heart.

You will always be one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.

And if I could go back to that day at Joy Sir's tuition and start the journey all over again...

I would.

Every single time.

Happy 23rd Birthday, Baccha ❤️

And before I end this letter, there is only one thing left to say...

Je tu meri jaan bhi mangle,

Main Taan Sajna Di Bahaan Vich Mukna Haye ❤️

Forever Yours,

Sanjay ❤️
